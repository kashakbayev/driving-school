Training program

Three stages from theory to the final test

  1. Online theory

    Traffic law and road signage, vehicle mechanics basics and first aid. Theory classes run online, so you build a clear foundation before practical driving.

  2. Practical driving

    Lessons with an instructor in a dual-control car: vehicle control, parking, city and highway driving at a closed training facility, on the streets of Astana and on the Astana–Burabay highway.

  3. Final test

    You finish the course with a final test. If you do not pass it, you get two free retake lessons before the next exam attempt.
