Course Detail: Category B (Car)

Our most popular course, Category B prepares you for the standard passenger-car RK driving license. Jump straight to requirements or to the booking form if you already know this is the course for you.

Curriculum

The course combines classroom theory with hands-on practice in a dual-control training car. All lessons focus on the skills needed for a Category B driving license.

  • Traffic law and road signage
  • Vehicle mechanics basics
  • First aid
  • Practical driving: city, highway, parking

Requirements

  • Minimum age: 17 (license issued at 18)
  • Valid medical certificate
  • Two passport-style photos

Quick Tips & FAQ

Q: Can I switch from manual to automatic mid-course?
A: Yes, with instructor approval before week 3.

Q: What happens if I fail the final test?
A: You get two free retake lessons before the next exam attempt.
