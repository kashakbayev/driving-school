Welcome to Drive & Go

Welcome to Drive & Go, an Astana driving school focused on helping new drivers become calm, careful, and confident on the road. Learning to drive can feel exciting and a little stressful at first, so our goal is to make each step clear and manageable. Students learn the rules of the road through theory lessons and then practice those ideas during real driving sessions with experienced instructors. We work on important skills such as observation, safe turns, lane changes, parking, speed control, and sharing the road with other drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians. Drive & Go also helps students understand the licensing process, from the learner’s permit to the final road test. Our instructors encourage questions and give practical feedback so students can improve one skill at a time. Whether you are starting your first driving course, returning after a break, or looking to become more comfortable behind the wheel, we want you to learn at a steady pace and build safe habits that will stay with you long after your test.
