Welcome to Prestige

Welcome to Prestige, an Astana driving school focused on helping new drivers become calm, careful, and confident on the road. We provide training for a Category B driving license. Students learn the rules of the road through theory lessons and then practice those ideas during real driving sessions with experienced instructors. We work on important skills such as observation, safe turns, lane changes, parking, speed control, and sharing the road with other drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians. Prestige also helps students understand the licensing process, from the learner’s permit to the final road test. Our instructors encourage questions and give practical feedback so students can improve one skill at a time. Whether you are starting your first driving course, returning after a break, or looking to become more comfortable behind the wheel, we want you to learn at a steady pace and build safe habits that will stay with you long after your test.

Our first lesson rule is slow hands, calm eyes because careful habits begin before the engine starts.
