Weekly Class Schedule

Drive & Go offers flexible driving lessons and online theory classes. The school teaches the Class 5 driving program and helps students follow the Road Safety Education Program. Theory lessons are online, while practical lessons are available at the Astana-Burabay highway and Astana streets. Electric vehicles can also help reduce CO2 emissions during training.
