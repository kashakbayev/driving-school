Weekly Class Schedule

Prestige offers flexible Category B driving lessons and online theory classes. The school teaches the Category B driving program and helps students follow the Road Safety Education Program. Theory lessons are online, while practical lessons are available at the Astana-Burabay highway and Astana streets. Electric vehicles can also help reduce CO2 emissions during training.
