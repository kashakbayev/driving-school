How to Enroll

Already spoken with our office by phone? Continue from where you left off:

  1. Submit the application form (in person or online)
  2. Provide required documents: ID and medical certificate
  3. Pay the registration fee
  4. Attend the orientation session
  5. Begin theory classes

What Each Course Covers

  • Theory
    • Traffic rules
    • Road signs
    • First aid basics
  • Practical
    • Vehicle control
    • Parking
    • City driving
